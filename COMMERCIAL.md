# Commercial license

GitBonsai is free for personal and other noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Commercial use needs a commercial license.

## What counts as commercial use

Any use for work at or for a company is commercial use. That includes employees using GitBonsai on their job, contractors and freelancers using it for client work, and use on company-owned or company-managed machines. The size of the company does not matter.

Personal projects, study, hobby work and work for a charity, school or public institution are noncommercial and need no license, as the PolyForm Noncommercial License describes.

## Evaluation

You may evaluate GitBonsai for commercial use for 30 days without a license. After that, buy a license or stop using it for work.
